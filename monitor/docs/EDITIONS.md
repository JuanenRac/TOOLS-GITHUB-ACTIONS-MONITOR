# Complete and Lite: what is in each edition

There are two editions in one package. **Lite** shows what runs, the failures of the last 24 hours, the history and basic charts: no sentinel, no network mode, none of the modules below, and it writes nothing to GitHub. **Complete** has everything else. There is no third edition: what writes to GitHub or reaches outside is in Complete, switched off until you turn it on. The modes of use of Complete (one person, a team, a service) are in their own guides.
